Skincare Blog

Helpful information about body lotions, body oils and simple everyday skincare routines.

Choosing a body lotion

Different skin types can benefit from different moisturisers. Consider your skin's needs, product ingredients and how the lotion feels on your skin. Follow product instructions and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Why moisturising matters

Moisturisers can help support the skin's natural protective barrier and reduce moisture loss. Applying a suitable moisturiser after bathing can be a useful part of an everyday routine.

How to use body oils

Depending on the product, body oils can help soften the skin and support moisture retention. Read the label and follow the recommended directions for use.

Building a simple skincare routine

A simple routine can include gentle cleansing, moisturising and sunscreen on exposed skin during the day. Choose products that suit your needs and introduce new products carefully.
